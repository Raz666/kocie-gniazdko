# Retusz fotografii — wariant 04

Wykonano wbudowanym narzędziem image_gen, na podstawie oryginalnych fotografii pensjonatu. Są to warianty z retuszem generatywnym na potrzeby makiety: model może zmieniać drobne szczegóły, więc nie traktujemy ich jako wiernej dokumentacji fotograficznej obiektu. Oryginalne zdjęcia pozostają bez zmian. Finalne PNG oraz mniejsze wersje WebP znajdują się w tym katalogu; HTML osadza WebP i działa bez katalogu zasobów.

## Zdjęcie ogrodowe

Źródło: `../photos/photo-20.jpg`. Wynik: `garden-cat-retouched.png`, `garden-cat-retouched.webp`.

Prompt:

Use case: lighting-weather. Asset type: editorial hero photograph for the real small cat boarding house Kocie Gniazdko. Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET, not just inspiration.
Primary request: carefully retouch this exact photograph into a softly lit, warm, contemporary hospitality editorial photograph. Keep the exact same dark fluffy cat, facial identity, yellow-green eyes, pose, body proportions, birch climbing frame, mesh enclosure, white building, window and all existing plants. No replacement architecture, no additional objects or cats.
Lift the underexposed cat's face gently so its eyes and fur are readable. Recover harsh whites, reduce acidic yellow/lime saturation in foreground leaves toward warm natural olive greens, balance exposure with soft warm daylight and natural cream highlights. Preserve believable fur and plant textures. Very subtle background depth of field, still recognizable. Keep the subject crisp without HDR or excessive sharpening. Preserve existing composition, landscape approximately 4:3. Retouch away only the old overlaid Kocie Gniazdko website logo/watermark in the lower right, seamlessly restoring the existing leaves there; the real logo is supplied separately in the website header. No new text, frames, graphic motifs or stylization. This should look like a professional gentle color correction of the same real photograph, not a generated luxury hotel scene.

Prompt:

Use case: lighting-weather. Input image is the exact EDIT TARGET: photograph of the actual Kocie Gniazdko cat by the window. Create a gentle professional photographic retouch for a warm contemporary botanical hospitality website. Preserve exactly the same fluffy tabby cat's facial identity, pose, eyes, body, fur markings and proportions. Preserve window, curtain, wood sill, chair, background mesh and greenery. Keep landscape 4:3 composition. Soften the harsh daylight and clipped highlights on the sill and curtain, warm neutral tones into cream and honey wood, moderate the greenery saturation into natural warm olive, delicately open deep shadows without flattening contrast. Preserve photographic texture. No room redesign, added furnishings, new objects, fake light beams or embellishments. Remove the small old overlaid website logo in the bottom left and restore the existing shadow/curtain behind it. No text, watermark or frame. Natural restrained editorial color correction of this same real cat in the same real room.
